Long bio:
Global LMS is a standards-centered K-12 curriculum warehouse with ready-to-load lessons, units, and full courses by country, grade, subject, and build type. Teachers can find editable curriculum quickly, then move it into Google Classroom, Canvas, Moodle, Schoology, Blackboard, Brightspace, Microsoft Teams, or any LMS. Built for teachers, families, schools, and global access.
