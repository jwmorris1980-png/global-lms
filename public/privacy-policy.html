Global LMS Privacy Policy

How Global LMS handles information when teachers, tutors, and parents use the site or a ChatGPT app connected to it.

Information We Collect

Global LMS may collect information you provide directly, such as country, state, grade, subject, lesson requests, and other form inputs. We may also collect technical information such as browser data, usage events, and timestamps to help the service work properly.

How We Use Information

  • To generate lessons, quizzes, and units.
  • To improve the quality and speed of the platform.
  • To maintain usage analytics and basic security.
  • To support account features and saved work where enabled.

ChatGPT App

If you use Global LMS through ChatGPT, the request data you send to the app may include country, state, grade, subject, and the draft type you ask for. That data may be transmitted to our backend only to generate the requested lesson, quiz, unit, or course. We do not intend to use that data for unrelated purposes.

Sharing

We do not sell personal data. We may share limited information with service providers needed to run the platform, such as hosting or analytics providers, subject to their own security and privacy practices.

Retention

We keep data only as long as needed to operate the service, support user requests, and meet legal or operational needs.

Contact

If you have privacy questions, contact the Global LMS owner through the site support channel or the contact information listed on the main website.
